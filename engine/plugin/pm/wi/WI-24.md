# WI-24 A Secretary decision could not be completed

The Secretary handles recovery; the decision already routed to the PM stays in its inbox.

## Trigger and evidence

A Secretary decision has no usable result: an older one-shot seat ended, was released or cancelled, or its hand-back was not accepted.
For a resident decision interrupted by replacement, the current round may still have an in-flight dispatch.
Evidence: escalation source, subject, options, round and dispatch state (`mp read chain <dispatch>`).

## Default action already taken

A failed legacy decision routes to the PM inbox; only its subject waits and unrelated work continues.
A replacement fences the old resident generation; it does not silently end that session or its dispatch.

## Options and outcomes

1. For an interrupted resident round, confirm the old seat ended, `mp sec release <D> --ended`, then claim the current round with `mp sec card <escalation>`; read its materials again and decide.
2. For a decision routed to the PM, leave it there (`ask-pm`); the PM decides from recorded words or asks the user under F-3.
3. The PM records `mp pm answer <mission> <escalation> --option <option> --by pm --words W-<n> --reason "..."`, or `--no-words --reason "..."` for the minor-impact default; a user's own answer uses `--by user --words W-<n>`.

Only an offered option can be applied.
A user answer overrides an unapplied PM answer; a completed decision is history, so a later change needs a repair, changed plan or new escalation.

## How to choose

Recover the resident round when its dispatch was interrupted; do not create another one-shot Secretary.
Do not take a routed PM-class question back into your own authority.

## When to send to the PM

Use `needs-pm` / `ask-pm` with `mp sec decide <escalation> ...` for any PM-class question; the PM decides on words or asks the user.
A failed legacy decision is already there; no duplicate question is needed.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
