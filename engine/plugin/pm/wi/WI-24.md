# WI-24 The Secretary could not decide

## Trigger and evidence
- The one-shot Secretary dispatched for an escalation ended without a usable decision: it was released without a hand-back (WI-28), its hand-back was not accepted (after a stop in scope, after its release), or its step was cancelled.
- Evidence: the escalation (source, subject, options), the Secretary dispatch's state and note (`mp read chain <dispatch>`).

## Default action already taken
The decision goes to you and the user. Only the escalated item waits (one plan step, one task or one exploration); the rest of the mission goes on. No second Secretary step is offered by itself (it could fail the same way).

## Options and outcomes
1. Record the decision: `mp pm answer <mission> <escalation> --option <option> [--instructions "<text>"] [--extra <n>] --words W-<n>` → `mp` carries it out exactly as it would the Secretary's. The options are the escalation's own: accept, accept-risk, send-back, replan, grant, restart, abandon, answer, rework. Accepting a negated review position records the user's ruling as a pass that revokes the negation.
- Until a decision is carried out, the user may override the Secretary's: `mp pm answer` is accepted, and the user's answer is the one carried out (an acceptance's ruling is written only when it is carried out).
- `mp` refuses an answer to an escalation already carried out (or closed as superseded): it names who decided it and with which option, and records nothing. To change course with the user, take the change to the plan (`mp pm plan submit` with their words), or answer the next escalation the flow raises.

## How to choose
You can decide within a recorded authorization (a detail, or an important decision the user authorized) → decide, then tell the user in one line; an important design decision → ask the user.

## When to ask the user
For important design decisions (a change to what they get, a choice hard to undo, a conflict with their words); and when the Secretary keeps failing.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites (reset, forced checkout) in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
