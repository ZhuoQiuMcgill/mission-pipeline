# WI-08 An automatic loop is exhausted, or repeats the same failure

## Trigger and evidence
- A loop of a lineage reached its limit (rework rounds, mechanical and feasibility returns, repeated dispatches of a step, delivery rebuilds, landing attempts), or the same failure signature came twice in a row (no progress; not for delivery rebuilds). A reopened task's attempts count on its rework loop, and a repair's returns on its mechanical-return loop.
- Evidence: the loop, the count, each failure signature.

## Default action already taken
Only this lineage stops at "exhausted"; the rest of the mission goes on. A Secretary step for it is ready in `mp pm next`.

## Options and outcomes
The Secretary picks first (dispatch its step); you relay the user's choice only after that:
1. The Secretary grants one extra round (at most 2 attempts, with a reason; once per lineage).
2. The Architect splits the task or changes approach (the Secretary's `replan`) → the new lineage inherits the old one's counts.
3. Give the task up; it goes on the risk list.
4. After the Secretary has granted once, only the user can add more: `mp pm grant <lineage> <loop> --extra <n> --words W-<n>`.

### A reopened task given up
- Trigger: an integration task's review reopened a task (notice `reopened-task-abandoned`), and that task was then given up (the Secretary or the user chose to give it up, usually after its own loop was used up).
- Default: only the integration task waits for it; the rest of the mission goes on.
- Options: a re-plan (`mp pm plan submit`) or a repair (`mp pm repair <mission> --words W-<n> --kind defect --text "<the user's reason, verbatim>" --about <task-id>`) routes the work the reopen asked for. Either needs the user's words; tell them what the integration found.

### Explorations
- Trigger: the round budget (default 3 attack rounds, set in the definition) is used and the exploration has not converged, the Stabilizer judging the remaining in-scope findings undeviated; a fatal finding of the same class was not fixed in two consecutive rounds; or two rounds ended with the same unresolved findings and nothing new. A finding the Stabilizer judged answered (the author's revision or rebuttal resolves it) counts as resolved, so a last round whose answers it accepts converges instead. Evidence: the findings, the author's dispositions and the Stabilizer's judgments (`mp read show exploration <id>`).
- Default: no further round is ready for this exploration; an escalation went to the Secretary with the findings, dispositions and judgments.
- Options (the Secretary picks; the user when asked, `mp pm answer <mission> <escalation> --option ... --words W-<n>`): `grant` 1 or 2 more rounds (the Secretary's one extension; after it, only the user); `accept-risk` (the current version with its unresolved findings as residual risks); `send-back` with a new direction; `replan` (settles as "no conclusion"; a direction decision: ask the user); `abandon`.
- A settled exploration reopened on your word after an environment change (`mp pm reopen-exploration`, notice `exploration-reopened`: its experiments run again and the attacker re-checks), or reopened by a repair for one more round, that stops again is this WI like a first stop: the same options; `accept-risk` settles it again on the ruled version, `replan` or `abandon` settle it as "no conclusion", and the decision layer hears the new result (its earlier conclusion is history).
- An exploration that ends without a conclusion that stands (given up, re-planned, or a research answer that does not stand): its routing escalation offers `replan`. Once it is decided (the Secretary, or the user: `mp pm answer <mission> <escalation> --option replan --words W-<n>`), the notice `exploration-no-conclusion` asks you to re-plan the part that waited for it with the user (a new exploration id or another approach, `mp pm plan submit`).
- A seat that asks for more evidence executions in one dispatch than allowed (default 3): the extra ones are recorded as not run, and its next card says so.
- Choose: in-scope findings shrinking round by round → grant; the same fatal class keeps coming back, or no progress → send-back or replan, not more rounds; acceptable remaining risks → accept-risk. A finding the Stabilizer judged a shift of direction is WI-33, not a reason for more rounds.

## How to choose
The same failure signature → change approach rather than add attempts; a step that failed for a reason outside the task (a tool missing on this machine) → fix that first, then grant.

## When to ask the user
After the Secretary has granted once; for `replan` of an exploration.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites (reset, forced checkout) in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
