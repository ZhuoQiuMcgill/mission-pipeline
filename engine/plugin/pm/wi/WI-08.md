# WI-08 An automatic loop is exhausted, or repeats the same failure

The Secretary handles this page.
A choice needing the user's words or a PM-class decision goes to the PM inbox: use `needs-pm` / `ask-pm` on the escalation with `mp sec decide <escalation> ...`.
The PM decides on recorded words or asks the user under F-3; the PM runs the words-dependent `mp pm` actions below.

## Trigger and evidence

- A loop of a lineage reached its limit (rework rounds, mechanical and feasibility returns, repeated dispatches of a step, delivery rebuilds, landing attempts), or the same failure signature came twice in a row (no progress; not for delivery rebuilds). A reopened task's attempts count on its rework loop, and a repair's returns on its mechanical-return loop.
- Evidence: the loop, the count, each failure signature.
- Also: a task waits for a re-plan nobody routed (notice `replan-waits`), or for a dependency that was given up (notice `dependency-abandoned`). See the sections below.

## Default action already taken

Only this lineage stops at "exhausted"; the rest of the mission goes on. Its decision is ready in `mp sec next`; open it with `mp sec card <escalation>`.

## Options and outcomes

The Secretary reads and decides the current round with `mp sec card`, `mp sec read` and `mp sec decide`; route PM-class options with `needs-pm` / `ask-pm`:

1. The Secretary grants one extra round (at most 2 attempts, with a reason; once per lineage).
2. The Architect changes approach (the Secretary's `replan` within the plan; a split or other structure change needs PM approval) → the new lineage inherits the old one's counts.
3. Give the task up only within the plan; dropping a task or deliverable is PM-class and goes to `ask-pm`; abandoned work stays on the risk list.
4. Accept the remaining risk (`accept-risk`, offered when the attempt has a verified hand-back, on a used-up rework loop and on a Reviewer's `needs-decision`; not on a re-review or a re-bind, whose product is accepted already): the attempt's product is accepted as it is, and the open findings are quoted on the acceptance as risks. The decider's ruling (the Secretary's, PM's, or user's with `mp pm answer <mission> <escalation> --option accept-risk --by user --words W-<n>`) overrules the Reviewer's position; no Reviewer pass is written. It goes through the same dependency gate as a pass: while a dependency is reopened or still in review, the decision waits, and it takes effect once that dependency is accepted again. If the attempt has moved on meanwhile, nothing is accepted and the decision is closed (notice `accept-risk-moot`).
   A review's reopen of a dependency with a used-up rework loop also offers the Secretary, PM and user `accept-risk`: the dependency keeps its accepted product; the integration's reviewed attempt is accepted as it is, with its open findings quoted as risks; no further review round runs. A repair's reopen does not offer it. Every dependency must still be accepted at the version reviewed, else the decision waits and is checked every pass (`accept-risk-waits`). When no integration waits for that reopen any more, or another grant advanced the dependency, nothing is accepted and the decision closes (`accept-risk-moot`).
5. After the Secretary has granted once, send it to the PM: `mp pm grant <lineage> <loop> --extra <n> --by pm --words W-<n>`, or a user grant on their words.

Several reopen escalations on one dependency attempt are decided separately. Grants ensure one next attempt, with every granted finding and request on its card; it waits for the other open choices. Abandon drops only that escalation's covered requests, preserving other grants. A re-plan routes its request without replacing another grant's next attempt; any structural revision still waits for PM approval. Sending it to the PM keeps the next attempt waiting.

### A re-plan nobody routed
- Trigger: notice `replan-waits`. A decision chose `replan` for a task, and no Architect attempt took it. A re-plan is routed in the same write when the decision layer is idle; this notice says what the routing waits on instead.
- Default: only that task and the work that depends on it wait.
- Options: do what the notice names (usually a PM plan batch, `mp pm plan submit`, with the user's words), or a repair that changes the task's card (`mp pm repair <mission> --about <task> ...`): the Architect changes the card, and that also answers the re-plan.

### A dependency given up
- Trigger: notice `dependency-abandoned`. A task waits for a dependency that was given up: there is no accepted product to start on.
- Default: only that task and the work after it wait.
- Options: a repair that changes the card of the waiting task or of the dependency (`mp pm repair`, with the user's words), or a re-plan. Send the PM the blocked work and the offered exits.

### A reopened task given up
- Trigger: an integration task's review reopened a task (notice `reopened-task-abandoned`), and that task or the reopen request was then given up (the Secretary, PM or user chose to give it up, usually after its loop was used up).
- Default: an integration without new accepted dependency work waits; other grants and the rest of the mission go on.
- Options: a re-plan (`mp pm plan submit`) or a repair (`mp pm repair <mission> --words W-<n> --kind defect --text "<the user's reason, verbatim>" --about <task-id>`) routes the work the reopen asked for. Either needs the user's words; send the integration findings to the PM.

### Explorations
- Trigger: the round budget (default 3 attack rounds, set in the definition) is used and the exploration has not converged, the Stabilizer judging the remaining in-scope findings undeviated; a fatal finding of the same class was not fixed in two consecutive rounds; or two rounds ended with the same unresolved findings and nothing new. An exploration converges by its state, in any round, the budget's last or a granted one included: the Stabilizer judged the round on track, no direction question waits, and every in-scope fatal or serious finding is resolved (by a re-check, or judged answered). No closing attacker runs. The budget only limits whether another round starts. Evidence: the findings, the author's dispositions and the Stabilizer's judgments (`mp read show exploration <id>`).
- Default: no further round is ready for this exploration; an escalation went to the Secretary with the findings, dispositions and judgments.
- Options (the Secretary picks within the plan; the PM decides its class on words or asks the user, `mp pm answer <mission> <escalation> --option ... --by user --words W-<n>`):
  - `grant` 1 or 2 more rounds (the Secretary's one extension; after it, the PM or user). A grant may carry instructions for the next round's cards (`--instructions "..."`) and name the findings it is for, general ones too (`--findings <id>,<id>`): the author then gets a turn on them;
  - `accept`, offered when nothing fatal or serious is unresolved and no direction question waits (it then replaces `accept-risk`): the latest version stands on the decider's ruling, with no risk list;
  - `accept-risk` (the current version with its unresolved findings as residual risks);
  - `send-back` with a new direction (PM-class: `ask-pm`);
  - `replan` (settles as "no conclusion"; a direction decision: send the question to the PM);
  - `abandon`.
- A settled exploration reopened on your word after an environment change (`mp pm reopen-exploration`, notice `exploration-reopened`: its experiments run again and the attacker re-checks), or reopened by a repair (its author gets the turn at once), that stops again is this WI like a first stop: the same options; `accept-risk` settles it again on the ruled version, `replan` or `abandon` settle it as "no conclusion", and the decision layer hears the new result (its earlier conclusion is history).
- An exploration that ends without a conclusion that stands (given up, re-planned, or a research answer that does not stand): its routing escalation offers `replan`. The direction choice goes to the PM inbox (`ask-pm`); `exploration-no-conclusion` asks the PM to re-plan what waited for it, on recorded words or with the user (a new exploration id or another approach, `mp pm plan submit`).
- A seat that asks for more evidence executions in one dispatch than allowed (default 3): the extra ones are recorded as not run, and its next card says so.
- Choose: nothing left unresolved → accept; in-scope findings shrinking round by round → grant, naming the findings and saying what to do; the same fatal class keeps coming back, or no progress → send-back or replan, not more rounds; acceptable remaining risks → accept-risk. A finding the Stabilizer judged a shift of direction is WI-33, not a reason for more rounds.

## How to choose

The same failure signature → change approach rather than add attempts; a step that failed for a reason outside the task (a tool missing on this machine) → fix that first, then grant. Every standard met but one narrow finding, on a verified hand-back → accept-risk rather than more attempts. A repair whose mechanical returns are used up offers only a grant; it holds no other work, so leaving it is safe.

## When to send to the PM

Use `ask-pm` for the cases below; the PM decides on the words or asks the user.
After the Secretary has granted once; for a structural change, changed user result, irreversible choice, conflict with recorded words, or new exploration direction.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites (reset, forced checkout) in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
