# WI-16 A dependency cycle

## Trigger and evidence
- When `mp` computed the labels, a new judgment closed a dependency cycle (judgments and objects in a cycle, or prerequisites in a cycle without a proof unit).
- Evidence: the objects, judgments and edges on the cycle (`mp read why <id>` on any of them).

## Default action already taken
By the least fixed point, nothing on the cycle, and nothing that depends on it, is proven; stable-mode steps that need them wait; fast-mode steps and unrelated work go on.

## Options and outcomes
1. The modules really depend on each other: have the Architect make them a proof unit, accepted as a whole by an integration task (a PM plan batch that asks for it: `mp pm plan submit <mission> --file <plan.json> --words W-<n>`).
2. The cycle comes from an unnecessary edge: have the Architect adjust the decomposition or the cards (the same way), then the seats judge again.
3. Leave it for now: the objects on the cycle are delivered as not fully proven and go on the risk list.

## How to choose
The members really must be accepted together → 1; the edge was added by mistake → 2; not in the delivery → 3.

## When to ask the user
Usually not.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites (reset, forced checkout) in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
