# WI-16 A dependency cycle

The Secretary handles this page.
For an existing escalation, route a PM-class choice with `needs-pm` / `ask-pm` through `mp sec decide <escalation> ...`.
For a question without an escalation, use `mp sec ask-pm "<question>" [--about <notice or item id>]`; the PM answers with `mp pm reply <id> "<text>" [--words W-n]`, visible in `mp sec next` / `mp sec wait`.
The PM decides on recorded words or asks the user under F-3; the PM runs the words-dependent `mp pm` actions below.

## Trigger and evidence

- When `mp` computed the labels, a new judgment closed a dependency cycle (judgments and objects in a cycle, or prerequisites in a cycle without a proof unit).
- Evidence: the objects, judgments and edges on the cycle (`mp read why <id>` on any of them).
- Or (notice `after-cycle`): a PM plan in effect has explorations whose `after` entries wait for each other (A after B, B after A, or longer). Evidence: the cycle, listed in the notice; `mp sec next` shows each first step as held, naming the cycle. A new PM plan batch with such a cycle is refused (`AFTER_CYCLE`, nothing recorded), so this usually comes from a plan that took effect before 4.1.3.

## Default action already taken

By the least fixed point, nothing on the cycle, and nothing that depends on it, is proven; stable-mode steps that need them wait; fast-mode steps and unrelated work go on.

`after-cycle`: nothing is refused; the explorations of the cycle stay held and the rest of the plan goes on.

## Options and outcomes

1. The modules really depend on each other: The engine records no proof unit (no plan field or command creates one), so have the Architect put the mutually dependent work into one task (a PM plan batch that asks for it: `mp pm plan submit <mission> --file <plan.json> --words W-<n>`).
2. The cycle comes from an unnecessary edge: have the Architect adjust the decomposition or the cards (the same way), then the seats judge again.
3. Leave it for now: the objects on the cycle are delivered as not fully proven and go on the risk list.
4. `after-cycle`: submit a PM plan without one of the `after` entries of the cycle (`mp pm plan submit <mission> --file <plan.json> --words W-<n>`). Keep the entry that says which result really builds on which.

## How to choose

The members really must be accepted together → 1; the edge was added by mistake → 2; not in the delivery → 3. An `after` cycle is always a mistake in the plan → 4.

## When to send to the PM

Use the escalation's `ask-pm` option if it has one; otherwise record the question with `mp sec ask-pm "<question>" --about <notice or item id>`. The PM decides on the words or asks the user.
For any structural plan revision, including merging tasks; for an `after-cycle` whose order was set by the user, send the conflicting order too.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites (reset, forced checkout) in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
