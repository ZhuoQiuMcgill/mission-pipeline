# WI-16 A dependency cycle

## Trigger and evidence
- The evaluator finds that a new judgment closes a dependency cycle (judgments and objects in a cycle, or prerequisites in a cycle without a proof unit). Evidence: the objects, judgments and edges on the cycle.

## Default action already taken
By the least fixed point, the objects and judgments on the cycle and everything depending on them do not hold; stable dispatch that needs them waits; fast dispatch and unrelated work go on.

## Options and outcomes
1. The two modules really depend on each other: have the Architect make a proof unit, accepted as a whole by an integration task (through the Secretary; no `mp` command yet: state the choice in the session).
2. The cycle comes from an unnecessary edge: have the Architect adjust the decomposition or cards, then the seats judge again (through the Secretary; no `mp` command yet: state the choice in the session).
3. Leave it for now: the objects on the cycle are delivered as not fully proven and go on the risk list.

## How to choose
Members really must be accepted together → 1; the edge was added by mistake → 2; not in the delivery → 3.

## When to ask the user
Usually not.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no repository hooks or forced rewrites in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
