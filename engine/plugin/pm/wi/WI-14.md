# WI-14 Cleanup keeps failing, or processes cannot be ended

## Trigger and evidence
- Cleanup stays pending for long; at a stop, processes are still alive after being killed; a child process is still alive 60 seconds after an action's timeout; a supervisor outlived its proof deadline and was ended.

## Default action already taken
Retrying with back-off; the stop report says "stop in force, but N processes cannot be ended" (`mp show stop <id>`); their resources stay counted, other work is admitted as usual; later operations in the same conflict domain wait for the child to exit.

## Options and outcomes
1. Retry (default, automatic).
2. Tell the user the manual step needed (e.g. reboot the machine).

## How to choose
Processes stuck in uninterruptible disk I/O that do not end for long → 2.

## When to ask the user
When a manual step is needed.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no repository hooks or forced rewrites in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
