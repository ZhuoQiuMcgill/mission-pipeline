# WI-33 An exploration awaits a ruling: the Stabilizer found a shift of direction or a drift

The Secretary handles this page.
A choice needing the user's words or a PM-class decision goes to the PM inbox: use `needs-pm` / `ask-pm` on the escalation with `mp sec decide <escalation> ...`.
The PM decides on recorded words or asks the user under F-3; the PM runs the words-dependent `mp pm` actions below.

## Trigger and evidence

- After an attack round, the Stabilizer judged a finding "shifts direction" (the finding, or the fix the author accepted, would change the question, the acceptance goal or the direction, or contradicts the user's words), or judged the round "drifting" (the product moved away from the definition across rounds).
- Evidence: the exploration, the round, the findings with the author's dispositions and the Stabilizer's judgments (`mp read show exploration <id>`).
- A round budget used up without convergence is WI-08.
- The same during the re-check of a settled exploration you reopened after an environment change (`mp pm reopen-exploration`, notice `exploration-reopened`): ending it settles it again as "no conclusion".

## Default action already taken

The exploration is "awaiting ruling": its version stays "pending" and cannot be accepted; the direction finding is not fixed; findings judged in scope still bind the author. A decision is ready in `mp sec next`. Other work goes on.

## Options and outcomes

1. Open the current decision with `mp sec card <escalation>`; read and decide within the plan, or choose `needs-pm` / `ask-pm` for a direction change.
2. The PM rules on recorded words or asks the user (WI-24); a user answer is recorded by the PM: `mp pm answer <mission> <escalation> --option <option> --by user --words W-<n>`:
   - keep the definition (`answer`, with the ruling as instructions) → the finding is set aside and the rounds go on;
   - change the direction (`send-back` with the new direction) → the next round works to it; a change of the question or the acceptance goal itself is a new PM plan batch (`mp pm plan submit`);
   - end it (`abandon`) → the exploration ends without a conclusion.
3. Drifting: the same; a `send-back` that restates the definition is the usual ruling.

After the ruling, the round ends by its state: when nothing else is open, it converges at once. When the round's own verdict was left pending by the question, or a ruling landed while its Stabilizer was judging, one new Stabilizer judges the same round again from a current card. No round is spent and no attacker is called in.

## How to choose

Put the finding beside the recorded definition; keep within-plan details with the Secretary and send a change of question, acceptance goal or direction to the PM.
Do not presume the answer.
Repeated drift is evidence for the PM's choice, not a reason to ask the user yourself.

## When to send to the PM

Use `needs-pm` / `ask-pm` with `mp sec decide <escalation> ...` for a direction change, conflict with the user's words or irreversible choice.
The PM decides on recorded words or asks the user under F-3.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
