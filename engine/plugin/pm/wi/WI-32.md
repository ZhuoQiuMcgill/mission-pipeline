# WI-32 Recomputing the labels does not finish

## Trigger and evidence
- A large change (a withdrawn requirement item, a refuted version with many dependents) left label work that `mp` does in bounded batches across its calls, and the stored frontier has stayed non-empty for 10 minutes: new large changes keep arriving faster than the batches.
- Evidence: `mp read status` shows "recomputing" and how much is left.

## Default action already taken
Every decision that needs "proven" (a stable-mode dispatch, an acceptance, a closing, a delivery, a legalization) waits, for the whole project, reporting how much is left; reads show the last labels marked "recomputing". Stops are never held back. Every `mp` call still does one batch.

## Options and outcomes
1. Stop dispatching new work, let the seats in flight hand back, then `mp pm drain` → it works through batches until the frontier is empty; the waiting decisions go on.
2. Meanwhile, dispatch only steps that do not need "proven" (fast mode) → their results are marked not fully proven; 1 is still needed later.

## How to choose
Normally 1. When the large changes come from the user reshaping the plan, finish that first, then 1.

## When to ask the user
Usually not.

The safety floor is never an option: stops stay in force; nothing unproven is called proven; no forced rewrites (reset, forced checkout) in the user's worktrees; no deletion of the user's data.
