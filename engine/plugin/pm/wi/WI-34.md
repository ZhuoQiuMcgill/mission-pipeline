# WI-34 A plan's current pass judgments do not cover its applicable object constraints

The Secretary handles continuation after the PM resolves this gap.
This notice is already a PM inbox item; do not create a second `ask-pm` question.
The PM decides on recorded words or asks the user under F-3 and runs the words-dependent actions below.

## Trigger and evidence

- A PM plan or detailed plan in effect has current deciding pass judgments, but their recorded checks do not cover the object constraints applying to that plan now. No review of that plan is queued or running.
- The notice names the plan, uncovered constraint lines, versions and paths, and the affected review positions (Calibrator ① on the PM plan; feasibility and Calibrator ② on the detailed plan). Evidence: `mp read show object <plan-id>`, its deciding judgments, and the WI-34 notice in `mp sec alerts`.
- A constraint recorded while a review runs, or after its pass, can cause this. A constraint arriving while the review is queued is selected by the rebuilt card instead.

## Default action already taken

Stable production waits. Fast mode proceeds with the plan and its dependent products shown as "not fully proven"; contested or negated production still waits. No constraint is relaxed and no judgment is written by the program. No planning re-check starts automatically (Appendix I). One notice is sent per plan and uncovered set, retried if interrupted and closed when the gap is gone or the plan is replaced. If the same set recurs after its notice closed, a new notice is sent.

## Options and outcomes

This notice is a PM inbox item. Send the uncovered positions and costs to the PM; it obtains and records the user's new words for either exit below. Preserve the plan's current mode.

1. **PM-plan gap only:** resubmit the same PM plan document with those new words: `mp pm plan submit <mission> --file <unchanged-plan.json> --words W-<n> --mode stable` (use `--mode fast` if that is its current mode). After the PM submits it, use `mp sec next` and dispatch its Calibrator ① step with `mp sec dispatch <task>`.
   - This opens the Appendix I re-check on the same PM-plan version. A passing current check covering the constraints restores its proof and indirect dependents whose own positions remain current. It costs a Calibrator ① seat, possibly a full review if the continuation is refused; it creates no Architect, feasibility or Calibrator ② step. It cannot repair a detailed plan's own uncovered positions.
2. **Detailed-plan gap:** with the user's new words, edit the PM plan document to express the agreed change and submit a changed version: `mp pm plan submit <mission> --file <changed-plan.json> --words W-<n> --mode stable` (or its current `fast` mode). After the PM submits it, use `mp sec next` and `mp sec dispatch <task>` for each planning step; a structural revision still needs PM approval.
   - This creates a new PM-plan version and decomposition, with feasibility and Calibrator ② as applicable. The new detailed plan binds the current constraints. Kept accepted products can require new re-bound versions and Reviewer seats in stable mode; changed task cards require Constructors, declared runs and Reviewers. Old plans and judgments remain history. Merely resubmitting an unchanged plan cannot repair the old detailed plan's own positions.

## How to choose

Use option 1 when only the PM-plan position has the gap. Use option 2 when feasibility or Calibrator ② has the gap, or when the user also changes the plan. If both plans have gaps, option 1 may expose the detailed plan's gap after the PM plan recovers; option 2 obtains a new detailed plan. Do not manufacture a cosmetic change without the user's direction.

## When to send to the PM

Use the notice's existing PM inbox item; the PM decides on the words or asks the user.
Both exits need the user's words. Send the uncovered constraints and extra review or implementation cost to the PM for a re-check or an agreed changed plan. Automatic re-reviews, and re-reviewing feasibility or Calibrator ② in place, remain **Proposal, pending ruling**.
